Insured, Bonded and 22 Years Serving Mesa

22 years in commercial cleaning placements across the Mesa and East Valley market. In that time, the single most consistent question from facilities managers evaluating a new vendor is: "What happens if something goes wrong?" Here's the full answer.

General liability insurance

We carry general liability insurance appropriate for commercial cleaning operations. This covers property damage caused by a crew member during the course of a cleaning visit. If a crew member breaks a piece of equipment, damages a surface, or causes property loss, a claim runs through our coverage — not yours. A certificate of insurance is included with every written quote. If your property manager or lease requires a specific coverage amount, let us know at the quote stage and we'll confirm whether our policy meets that threshold.

Workers' compensation

Every crew member placed on your site is covered under a workers' compensation policy. If a crew member is injured while cleaning your facility, the claim goes through our workers' comp coverage. Your facility's general liability is not exposed to a third-party injury claim from our staff. This is a standard requirement that many cleaning vendors in this market either don't carry or don't maintain consistently. We do.

Bonding

We are bonded. Bonding provides coverage for verified theft by a crew member while on your premises. The bond is in place on every account, not selectively applied. If a crew member is found to have stolen property during a cleaning visit, the claim is handled through the bond.

22 years in business

Mesa Recurring Cleaning has been placing commercial cleaning crews in the East Valley for 22 years. That's not a marketing figure — it reflects a track record in this specific market with the logistics, crew networks, and facility types it contains. Insurance certificates, bonding documentation, and references from existing accounts are available on request through a written quote engagement.
