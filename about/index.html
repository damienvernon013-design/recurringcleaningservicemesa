About Mesa Recurring Cleaning

22 years placing commercial cleaning crews in Mesa and East Valley facilities. We are a lead-generation and contractor placement service — not a franchise and not a staffing agency. We match verified, insured cleaning crews to commercial accounts and manage the relationship between them.

What we do

When a Mesa facility manager calls us, they're not hiring an employee. They're engaging a service that handles crew sourcing, vetting, scheduling, scope documentation, and ongoing quality management. The difference matters because:

  • You're not responsible for employment logistics — crew selection, compliance, workers' comp exposure, payroll.
  • If a scheduled crew can't make a visit, we cover the shift. You don't manage a no-show.
  • Scope changes go through us. You're not renegotiating directly with a crew every time a task needs adjusting.

Who we work with

Our client base is commercial: offices, medical practices, dental suites, retail spaces, light industrial facilities, and property management companies overseeing multi-tenant buildings. We work with single-location businesses and multi-site portfolio managers.

We do not service residential properties. If you're looking for a home cleaning service, this isn't us.

Coverage and credentials
