Weekly & Bi-Weekly Cleaning in Mesa, AZ

For offices and commercial facilities that need a reliable clean on a fixed schedule. Weekly cleaning suits high-traffic environments — open-plan offices, medical waiting areas, retail sales floors. Bi-weekly is appropriate for lower-traffic spaces or facilities with strong internal housekeeping between visits.

What the service covers

Scope is set during a walkthrough before the contract starts. Standard scope for a weekly or bi-weekly commercial clean includes:

  • Hard floor vacuuming and mopping, including under desks and workstation bases
  • Restroom sanitisation — fixtures, surfaces, mirrors, restocking of consumables you supply
  • Kitchen and break room — surfaces, appliance exteriors, sink, floor
  • Trash removal and liner replacement to your bins
  • Reception and common area dusting, including horizontal surfaces and window ledges
  • Glass entry doors and interior glass partitions

Anything outside standard scope is listed separately in the written scope document. No surprises on the invoice.

Frequency and scheduling

Visits happen on a fixed day confirmed at contract start. If your business requires access to the building, we schedule around your operating hours. Most Mesa office clients run after-hours cleans — typically between 6pm and 10pm — to avoid disrupting staff.

Weekly cleaning contracts run month-to-month or on an annual basis. We'll advise which fits your situation.
