Weekly & Bi-Weekly Cleaning in Fountain Hills, AZ

Recurring office and commercial cleaning on a fixed weekly or bi-weekly schedule for Fountain Hills facilities. The same crew, the same scope, the same visit day — every cycle.

A Fountain Hills example

A 3,800 sq ft multi-physician medical practice on Shea Boulevard with five exam rooms, two physician offices, a check-in desk, a lab room, and two restrooms. Weekly cleans run Saturday mornings with key access provided to the crew lead.

How scheduling works in Fountain Hills

We confirm a fixed visit day at contract start. Most Fountain Hills facilities run after-hours cleans — typically after 6pm — to avoid disruption during operating hours. If your building has specific access requirements, we coordinate those directly with your facilities contact before the first visit.

Visit frequency is agreed upfront. If your facility's needs change mid-contract — headcount increases, layout changes, a new tenant — we adjust the scope in writing before the next invoice.

What's included

  • Full vacuum and mop of all hard and carpeted floors
  • Restroom sanitisation and restocking of consumables you supply
  • Kitchen and break room reset — surfaces, sink, appliance exteriors, floor
  • Trash removal and liner replacement
  • Dusting of horizontal surfaces, window ledges, and partition tops
  • Glass entry doors and interior glass partitions

Anything outside this scope is documented separately. Nothing extra appears on an invoice without your prior approval.
