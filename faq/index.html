Recurring Cleaning Service FAQs for Mesa Businesses

How is a recurring contract different from booking individual cleans?

A recurring contract sets a fixed schedule, assigns a consistent crew, and locks in a written scope and price. You don't re-book, re-brief, or re-negotiate before each visit. The crew knows your building. The price is the price.

Do you work with residential homes?

No. We work exclusively with commercial facilities — offices, medical practices, retail spaces, light industrial, and multi-tenant properties. If you're looking for a home cleaning service, we're not the right fit.

What areas do you cover?

Mesa and the surrounding East Valley. Our active service area includes Gilbert, Tempe, Chandler, Apache Junction, Scottsdale, Queen Creek, Higley, and Fountain Hills. All towns are within our 25-mile service radius from Mesa.

What happens if a scheduled crew can't make a visit?

We cover it. A briefed backup crew handles the visit. We notify you in advance when possible. You don't manage a no-show — we do.

Can I change the scope after the contract starts?

Yes. Scope changes are documented in writing and confirmed before implementation. If an area is added, the price adjusts accordingly. If an area is removed, so does the cost. Nothing changes without your sign-off.

How long is the contract term?

Contracts run month-to-month or on an annual basis. Annual contracts carry a lower per-visit rate. Month-to-month provides flexibility if your facility situation is uncertain. We'll advise which fits at the quote stage.

What if a clean doesn't meet the agreed scope?

Report it to us by phone or email by end of next business day. We return within 48 hours to correct the issue at no charge.

Do you supply cleaning products and equipment?

Yes. Crews bring their own equipment and commercial cleaning products. If your facility requires specific products for regulatory or allergy reasons, note that in your quote request and we'll confirm compatibility.

Are your crews insured?

Yes. We carry general liability insurance and all crews placed on your site carry workers' compensation coverage. A certificate of insurance is available with every written quote. Full details here.

How do I get a quote?

Call (866) 958-8773 during Mon–Fri 8:30am–5:00pm, or submit a quote request. We respond within one business day with a written scope and price.
