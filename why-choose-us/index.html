Why Mesa Buildings Choose Us

A 510,000-person market has cleaning options. Here's what separates Mesa Recurring Cleaning from the alternatives a facility manager is comparing us against.

22 years in the market

Most cleaning services in Mesa are under five years old. A vendor who started last year hasn't navigated a contract through an Arizona summer with a crew that called out sick, or managed the logistics of a facility that changed access protocols mid-contract. 22 years means we've handled those situations — and have the crew relationships and backup coverage to handle them again.

Written scope before the first visit

The most common complaint from facilities managers switching cleaning vendors is "they agreed to X and billed for Y." We address that before it starts. You receive a written scope document before the first crew arrives. Everything in that document is in the price. Nothing outside it shows up on an invoice without your prior approval.

Price transparency

We publish our pricing approach on our pricing page. We don't give flat rates that don't reflect real scope, and we don't pad quotes to leave room for "negotiation." The number in the written quote is the number on the invoice unless the scope changes.

Crew continuity

Rotating through different cleaning staff every month means re-training constantly and getting variable results. We assign a specific crew to your account. If that crew can't make a visit, we cover it with a crew that has been briefed on your scope — not whoever's available that night.

Insurance that protects your facility

We carry general liability coverage and all crews placed on your site carry workers' compensation insurance. If something breaks or someone is injured on shift, the liability does not transfer to you. Full coverage details →

Direct line for issues

One phone number, one email. Not a ticketing system, not a chatbot. (866) 958-8773 reaches us during Mon–Fri 8:30am–5:00pm. Quality issues reported by end of next business day are corrected within 48 hours at no charge.
