Recurring Cleaning Service Pricing in Mesa, AZ

Commercial cleaning prices in Mesa are set by square footage, room count, floor types, cleaning frequency, and scope. Two offices of the same size can have significantly different costs depending on restroom count, floor surfaces, and access hours. We do not publish flat rates because a flat rate that covers a dental suite does not reflect the real cost of a warehouse break room.

What drives the cost

FactorEffect on price
Square footagePrimary driver. Larger spaces take longer crews or longer shifts.
Restroom countEach restroom adds significant time — sanitation is not skippable.
Floor typesCarpet adds vacuuming time. Hard tile with grout adds more. VCT with buffing is higher still.
FrequencyWeekly visits cost less per visit than bi-weekly; crews maintain standard rather than restoring from extended use.
Access windowAfter-hours access (6pm–10pm) is standard. Restricted windows or early-morning requirements affect crew scheduling costs.
Specialist tasksWindow cleaning, disinfectant treatments, or high-dust work are scoped separately.

What to expect in a written quote

We quote per visit or per month. The written quote you receive includes a line-item scope, the crew allocation, the visit duration estimate, and the price. It does not include an open-ended line for "additional time" or "materials" — everything is agreed before the first visit.

Get an accurate price

The fastest way to get a real number is to call or submit a quote request. Have your square footage, restroom count, and preferred frequency ready and we can give you a price range in the first conversation. A binding quote follows a walkthrough or a detailed scope call.
